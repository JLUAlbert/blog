title: 'The Wheel of Equality'
date: '2026-11-01T19:19:38+08:00'

---

What matters more, the chance, or final share?  
The contest crowns the strong, condemns the weak;  
Yet those who win bequeath their worldly fare,  
While those who lose inherit all they lack.

Thus privilege takes root and branches grow,  
And children bear the burdens of the past;  
What once was chance becomes a fixed tableau,  
As widening gulfs grow deeper, vast and fast.

Till buried wrath erupts in fire and flame,  
And blood-red revolutions rend the land;  
The old order falls, though none remain the same,  
And equal fortunes rise from violence's hand.

Yet time restores the wheel to turn once more:  
The new-born victors guard the unequal door.

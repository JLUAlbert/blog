date: '2026-11-03'

When you set your heart wholly upon a goal, 

and devote every ounce of your wisdom, strength, 

and being to its pursuit, 

with your mind unwavering and your eyes fixed on the destination, 

the whole universe conspires to help you make it come true.

当你认定一个方向，

以全部的智慧、勇气与力量，

心无旁骛地奔赴它时，

整个世界仿佛都会为你让路，

万物也终将成为你的助力。

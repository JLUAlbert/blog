> **Think independently. Write honestly. Learn endlessly. Live freely.**
>
> 独立思考，诚实书写，学无止境，自由生活。—— 这是我的格言，也是这个站点的起点。

My name is **Albert Chan（陈树强）**, and this is **Dongmu（东木）**, my personal digital garden.

I am from Huanggang, Hubei, China, and currently live in Harbin, Heilongjiang.

## Education

- **2017–2020** — Huanggang High School（黄冈中学）
- **2020–2024** — School of Economics, Jilin University（吉林大学经济学院）— Bachelor's degree in Public Finance（财政学）

## Interests

I enjoy sports, especially table tennis, as well as photography, reading, and films. I am also interested in society, economics, politics, and the institutions that shape our lives.

我关注的问题大致是：制度如何塑造人，人又如何在制度里自处。经济学的训练给了我一副冷眼看分配的镜子，读书和电影提醒我冷眼之外还有体温。

## Why Dongmu Exists

A personal digital garden for independent thought, reading, photography, and reflections on society.

Some entries may be essays; others may be reading notes, photographs, film reflections, or fragments of everyday life.

这里不追热点，不做流量的生意。文章可能是长文，也可能是读书笔记、照片、观影随感，或者生活里的一小片碎片——它们唯一的共同点，是都经过了自己的 thinking，而不是转述。
